Likely questions

How do you choose r?

Start small (4–16); raise it for harder or knowledge-heavy tasks; tune on validation.

Why is B initialised to zero?

So BA = 0 at the start: training begins exactly at the pretrained model.

Does LoRA slow inference?

Not once merged (W = W₀ + BA). Kept separate to hot-swap, it adds a small cost.

LoRA or adapters?

Both freeze the base. Adapters add sequential layers; LoRA is parallel and mergeable.

What does α do?

Scales the update by α / r, so changing r needs less re-tuning of the learning rate.

Why not the paper’s 10,000×?

That is GPT-3 175B with few adapted matrices; our model is ~350× smaller, all layers adapted.

The original LoRA paper adapted the attention q and v projections; QLoRA found that adapting all linear layers was needed to match full fine-tuning, which is what we did. Is QLoRA lower quality? The QLoRA paper reports matching 16-bit full fine-tuning on its benchmarks thanks to NF4 and double quantization; it is slower to train.
