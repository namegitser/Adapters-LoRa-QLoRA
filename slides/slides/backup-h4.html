Backup, only if asked. We measured perplexity on general text (WikiText-2) before and after the Alpaca fine-tuning from slide 6. Full fine-tuning raised it 2.3 %, LoRA only 1.0 %: LoRA forgot less, a small reproduction of Biderman et al. 2024. Careful with QLoRA: it is compared with its own 4-bit base, which already starts worse (13.87 vs 12.50). Training recovered part of the quantization loss, so its −1.4 % is not negative forgetting.
