One full copy per task

Ten fine-tuned 7 B models are ten 14 GB files to store and serve.

Catastrophic forgetting

Rewriting every weight for the new task can erase what the model already knew.

Do we really need to update all 7 billion numbers?

16 bytes/parameter accounting: Rajbhandari et al. 2020 (ZeRO)

Speaker A · 0:00–0:50. Hook: fully fine-tuning a 7 B model in fp16 with Adam needs about 16 bytes per parameter: 2 for the fp16 weights, 2 for the gradients, 12 for Adam's two fp32 moment estimates plus an fp32 master copy of the weights. 7 billion × 16 bytes ≈ 112 GB, before activations. One A100 has 80 GB. That is our own arithmetic; the 16-byte accounting is from the ZeRO paper. Two more costs: one full copy of the model per task, and catastrophic forgetting.
